Crime Category Guide

Simple meanings for the incident type groupings shown above.

Violent Crimes — incidents involving physical harm, threats, or force against a person (e.g. homicide, physical injury, kidnapping, threats).

Property Crimes — incidents involving theft, damage, or illegal use of someone else's property (e.g. theft, robbery, vandalism, trespassing).

Financial / Fraud Crimes — deception or scams committed for money (e.g. estafa, swindling, cybercrime scams).

Social / Public Order Crimes — incidents that disturb peace or a person's honor/reputation (e.g. alarms and scandals, harassment, defamation).

Special / Child-Related — cases specifically involving the welfare or protection of minors (e.g. BCPC cases, child support).

Other Incidents — reports that don't fall under the categories above (e.g. missing person, noise complaint, traffic, animal welfare).
